Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about internet speed testing and PingMaster Pro.

What can PingMaster Pro measure?

The app is designed to show internet performance metrics such as download speed, upload speed, ping, latency and jitter, along with supported network information.

Can I test Wi-Fi and mobile data?

You can use PingMaster Pro to check supported internet connections, including Wi-Fi and mobile data. Results depend on your device, network conditions and the test method.

Why does my speed change between tests?

Results can vary with network congestion, signal strength, router performance, server location, background activity and other conditions. Testing more than once can help you compare performance, but no single test tells the whole story.

What does ping mean?

Ping is a measure of how long a small request takes to travel to a destination and return. Lower ping can help with activities that need quick responses, such as online gaming and video calls.

Do I need to create an account?

The app description provided for this page states that an account is not required to perform speed tests. Please check the current Google Play listing and in-app privacy information for the latest details.
